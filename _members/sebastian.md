## Technical Profile

Sebastián is an undergraduate Mechatronics Engineering student at UNAM. His technical focus is rooted in the development of efficient, performance critical software for autonomous robotic systems. He prioritizes computational efficiency and resource optimization as foundational principles in his work.

### Core Expertise and Technical Interests

Sebastián's work bridges low-level embedded development with high-level autonomy frameworks, emphasizing clean, maintainable code and robust system architecture:

- **Performance-Critical Systems Programming**: Sebastián specializes in C and C++ development. His approach prioritizes deterministic behavior and minimal resource usage for embedded autonomy and control loops.

- **Embedded Linux & System Integration**: With extensive experience on low level systems, he develops and deploys software that runs on embedded devices. His expertise extends to Linux system administration, kernel configuration, and custom scripting, enabling him to optimize the entire software stack from the operating system up to the application layer.

- **Technical Documentation & Collaboration**: Committed to meticulous documentation as an integral part of the development process. Enjoys sharing knowledge with colleagues and values clear, open communication as essential to effective teamwork in research and competition environments.
