From April 16-18, 2026, all Pumas teams from our lab took part in the Mexican Robotics Tournament 2026, held in UTBIS, Puebla.

The PUMAS Team, proudly representing the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM), geared up with intense preparation and enthusiasm for the Mexican Robotics Tournament (TMR) 2026. This national competition, held in Universidad Tecnológica Bilingüe Internacional y Sustentable de Puebla (UTBIS Puebla) is a crucial platform for innovation and engineering talent, where Pumas students will showcase their autonomous designs and programming skills on Humanoids Soccer, Drones and . Their participation not only signifies the quest for a championship title but also an invaluable opportunity to exchange knowledge with other leaders in the field, reinforcing UNAM's commitment to the development of technology and the training of high-caliber future engineers.
