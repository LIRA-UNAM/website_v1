From September 21-26, the PUMAS Team, proudly representing the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM), geared up with intense preparation and enthusiasm for the International Micro Air Vehicle tournament, held in Strasbourg, France. This year, our lab decided to join forces with BUAP, UDLAP and INAOE to form a joint-team consortium. This international competition is a crucial platform for innovation and engineering talent, where UNAM students will showcase their autonomous designs and programming skills specifically within the challenging Drone category. Their participation not only signifies the quest for a championship title but also an invaluable opportunity to exchange knowledge with other leaders in the field, reinforcing UNAM's commitment to the development of technology and the training of high-caliber future engineers.
{% include float.html clear=true %}

Mexico team finished 6th place in the overall ranking at the International PUMAS team teamed up with UDLAP, BUAP, INAOE teams to form the Mexico team Consortium.
