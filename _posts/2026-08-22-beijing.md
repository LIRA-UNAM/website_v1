From August 22 to 26, 2026, Pumas humanoid team was invited to compete at the RCAP Beijing Masters 2026, held in Beijing, China. 

The PUMAS Team, proudly representing the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM), geared up with intense preparation and enthusiasm for the Robocup 2026, the most important robotics competition in the world. This event, held in Incheon (South Korea) is a crucial platform for innovation and engineering talent, where Pumas students showcased their autonomous designs and programming skills on Humanoid Soccer League. Their participation not only signifies the quest for a championship title but also an invaluable opportunity to exchange knowledge with other leaders in the field, reinforcing UNAM's commitment to the development of technology and the training of high-caliber future engineers.
