The PUMAS Team, proudly representing the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM), geared up with intense preparation and enthusiasm for the Mexican Robotics Tournament (TMR) 2025. This national competition is a crucial platform for innovation and engineering talent, where Pumas students will showcase their autonomous designs and programming skills specifically within the challenging Humanoid KidSize category. Their participation not only signifies the quest for a championship title but also an invaluable opportunity to exchange knowledge with other leaders in the field, reinforcing UNAM's commitment to the development of technology and the training of high-caliber future engineers.
