# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-users" %}Openings

We are always looking for motivated students and researchers to join and colaborate with LIRA.

## Research and social service

Members of the UNAM community are welcome to join us if they wish to perform their **social service** with us or work on a **thesis** or **research project**. Aswell, we are open to hosting students from other universities, both national and foreign, who are interested in conducting a research stay in our lab.

There's more information on how to reach to us in [contact](/contact/).

## Competitions

If you are interested in joining one of our competition teams, we will be posting here if an opening is available.
